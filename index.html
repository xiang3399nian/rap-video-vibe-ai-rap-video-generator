Make the subject easy to recognize

Look for one clear person with a visible face and natural lighting. Heavy filters, objects across the face and distant group photographs can make the subject harder to read. A sharp image with some room around the person gives a more useful starting point than a tightly cropped screenshot.

Consider the body as well as the face. A dance scene involves turns, arm movements and changes of position.
